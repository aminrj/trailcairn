The trail runs through the Tiveden forest in the south of Laxå municipality, Örebro
County — a wild, hilly landscape whose core is protected as Tiveden National Park. It is
one of the largest tracts of old-growth forest left in southern Sweden: dark pine woods
allowed to grow untouched, dramatic rift valleys, house-sized boulders dropped by the
ice, and clear woodland lakes tucked between the ridges.

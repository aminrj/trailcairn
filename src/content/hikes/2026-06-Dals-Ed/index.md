Dals-Ed lies in the Dalsland lake district of western Sweden, close to the Norwegian
border and the long, narrow lake Stora Le. The municipality counts more than 400 lakes
and is threaded by centuries-old pilgrim trails; to the north-west sits Tresticklan
National Park, one of the largest roadless old-growth forests in southern Sweden.

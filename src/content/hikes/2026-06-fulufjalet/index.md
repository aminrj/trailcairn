Fulufjället is a national park on a high, treeless mountain plateau in northern Dalarna,
close to the Norwegian border. It is best known for Njupeskär, Sweden's highest waterfall
with a drop of roughly 90 metres, and for Old Tjikko — a Norway spruce whose root system
is about 9,500 years old, making it one of the oldest living trees on Earth.
